StickyED Updater

Plug the reTerminal Sticky into this computer with a USB data cable. This page updates its firmware and sets up the SD card over the same cable.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge. If you opened it by double-clicking and Connect does nothing, serve it locally instead: python3 -m http.server -d sticky-tool 8000, then open http://localhost:8000.
STEP 1

Connect

Switch the Sticky on (hold the round side button for about 2 seconds) so it shows its home screen, then choose it in the list. It usually appears as “USB Single Serial” or “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”.

STEP 2

Update firmware

Installs the Sticky × Hermes firmware bundled in this page. Your books, settings and SD card are not touched.

STEP 3

SD card setup

Writes /hermes/config.json to the card inside the Sticky. It takes effect right away: Wi-Fi passwords move into the Sticky's own store and are removed from the file.

The computer running sticky-bridge, as seen from your Wi-Fi.
Same as token in bridge.toml.
Wi-Fi networks Tick “home” for networks the Sticky may sync on. Leave a password blank to keep the one already saved on the device.

Also sets the Sticky's clock to this computer's time.
